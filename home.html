Who We Are

Building Engineers,
Shaping Futures

CESA — the Computer Engineering Students Association — is the official student body of the Computer Engineering Department at R. C. Patel College of Engineering and Polytechnic, Shirpur.

We exist to bridge the gap between academic learning and real-world application, fostering a culture of innovation, collaboration, and continuous growth among students.

  • Technical skill development through hands-on activities
  • Leadership and management opportunities for students
  • Competitions, events, and inter-college engagements
  • Cultural and sports activities for holistic growth
